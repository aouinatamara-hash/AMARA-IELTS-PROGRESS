Day 3 Active Passage Practice

Passage: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence in Agriculture

Academic Section 1

Paragraph A

Modern farming is undergoing a technological revolution driven by machine learning algorithms and autonomous field machinery. Recent studies from Cambridge University indicate that precision sensors installed on agricultural tractors can reduce chemical pesticide consumption by up to 35 percent. While implementation costs remain a significant hurdle for small-scale landholders, industrial farms in Western Europe have established reliable systems that yield high efficiency gains.

Paragraph B

Furthermore, satellite monitoring software allows land operators to anticipate regional droughts and adjust irrigation protocols days before physical stress becomes visible on crops. Despite these technological strides, traditional agricultural labor remains essential for soft-fruit harvesting, where delicate handling is mandatory.

Questions 1–3: True / False / Not Given

1. Agricultural sensors on tractors lead to a reduction in chemical usage.

2. Small-scale farmers find precision AI tools inexpensive to purchase.

3. Autonomous robots have completely replaced human fruit pickers in Europe.
